Interpretation and limits / 模型含义与范围
This is a simplified adult male reference model for educational visualization. Colors are illustrative, and display scale is not a physical measurement calibration.
The ventricular-wall mesh combines both ventricular walls and does not permit independent selection of the left and right walls.
The right-ventricle and left-ventricle meshes are solid surfaces representing chamber spaces, not muscle tissue. They become visible by hiding the surrounding wall or isolating a cavity.
The right-atrium and left-atrium meshes show atrial walls; atrial cavities are not included.
Coronary branches are grouped into two selectable artery trees, not separately named branches.
The subset omits independently selectable valves, papillary muscles, pulmonary veins, the inferior vena cava, pericardium, and many surrounding structures. Several vessel ends are truncated.
The source release notes acknowledge missing or incorrect anatomical representations: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/data/bodyparts3d/LATEST/release_4.0.html
Source identifier and geometry checks do not constitute independent expert anatomical or clinical validation. This asset is not a diagnostic, treatment, physical measurement, or physiological simulation model.
